The lawyer’s steps from here
2002

Encana

  • Mom and Dad held shares in one company, Encana.
  • Each had one certificate.

Split

  • Encana split its shares two for one.
  • Each got a second certificate for the same number of shares. That’s why each has two Encana certificates.

Cenovus

  • Encana split into two companies. Encana kept the natural gas business, and a new company, Cenovus, took the oil business.
  • Shareholders got one Cenovus share for each Encana share and kept their Encana shares.

Ovintiv

  • Encana moved to the United States and became Ovintiv.
  • Every five Encana shares became one Ovintiv share.
  • Mom and Dad’s Encana shares haven’t been converted yet. Odyssey Trust converts them and tells us how many Ovintiv shares they get.

Ovintiv: easy

  • BMO Medallion stamped both forms after checking wills, probate and death certificates.
  • So Odyssey’s email asks for nothing else.
  • Each envelope has 6 items.

Ovintiv: done

  • Letters of Transmittal: signed and stamped.
  • Sale requests, US tax forms and cover letters: printed.
  • Four Encana certificates: in hand.

Ovintiv: call

  • Can Mom’s shares go to Dad’s estate?
  • Do they want any other papers?
  • Will they accept the W‑8BEN‑E tax form?
  • Where should the US dollar cheque go?

Ovintiv: mail

  • I sign the sale requests, tax forms and cover letters.
  • One envelope per parent, by registered mail.
  • Odyssey sells the shares, takes its fee and mails a US dollar cheque.

Cenovus: hard

  • Mom’s will was never probated.
  • So Computershare wants a bond: 3% of the shares’ value, about $815 in September 2026.
  • Mom’s envelope has 14 items. Dad’s has 9.

Cenovus: done

  • Transfer forms: stamped.
  • Declarations: sworn and sealed.
  • Certified copies: in hand.
  • Letters of instruction: drafted.

Cenovus: 30 days

  • Mom’s bond quote lasts 30 days from the date on Computershare’s letter.
  • In that time their new forms come by mail, I swear them, and everything goes back.

Cenovus: steps

  1. Call Computershare, but hold off on Mom’s quote.
  2. Mail Dad’s envelope.
  3. Get everything for Mom’s ready.
  4. Ask for her quote and finish inside 30 days.
Done so far
  • Finished: the six stamped and sealed forms
  • In hand: the certified copies and all six share certificates
  • Printed: the sale requests, tax forms and letters
  • Left: two calls, three bank cheques, Mom’s Waiver of Probate, then signing and mailing

1. Odyssey Ovintiv shares, no deadline

The lawyer calls Odyssey Trust. The questions are on the call sheet.

Then I sign → both Ovintiv envelopes go by registered mail → Odyssey sells the shares and mails a US dollar cheque.

2. Computershare Cenovus shares, no deadline

The harder call, with six questions. The two big ones: can Mom’s shares go straight to Dad’s estate, and can Dad’s account skip the bond?

Don’t ask for Mom’s bond quote yet. It starts the 30 days. Then Dad’s envelope goes by registered mail.

3. Mom’s 30 days Deadline: 30 days from Computershare’s letter

The worst part. Ask for Mom’s bond quote only when everything else is ready. Mail time uses some of the 30 days.

The new Waiver comes → the lawyer checks it → I swear it before a notary with a seal → I get the bond cheque → the envelope goes.
